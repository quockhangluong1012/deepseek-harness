# Agent Note: Center-track page seat

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-09-10-center-track-page-seat.zh.md)

## Problem

The frame offered exactly one place for "a surface of your own": `shell.overlay`, a click-through layer above every column. A surface that is a *destination* rather than a notification — a Workspace page, a document, a dashboard — therefore had to fake being an app-level page inside it, with a fixed mask, a centered panel, and `role="dialog"`, or replace `conversation` outright and delete every seat its occupant declares (session body, composer, hero, the whole input dock). Neither is what a page is: the first covers the navigation column the user still needs, and the second is a process-global unmount triggered by opening a page.

`ui-workspace-memory` took the first route, and paid for it twice: the page read as a modal, and its stylesheet named eight `--dsw-*` properties that exist nowhere in `ui-theme` (`--dsw-surface`, `--dsw-text`, `--dsw-border`, `--dsw-track`, `--dsw-accent`, `--dsw-danger`, `--dsw-overlay-mask`, `--dsw-z-overlay`), so every color resolved to a hardcoded light-mode literal and the page stayed white in dark mode.

## Decision

`ui-layout` declares a fifth child of `root`: `shell.page`, `single` cardinality at `root` scope, rendered by `AppFrame` in a page layer that shares the center track with the conversation.

- **One page at a time, drawn over the conversation.** The page layer is a grid item placed into the same cell as `.centerCol` (`grid-area: 1 / 2`), so it follows the sidebar's width without measuring it, and the center column's `overflow: hidden` clips it to the track. The sidebar and the right column keep their geometry and stay usable.
- **The conversation stays mounted underneath.** The slot renders nothing while unoccupied, and an occupant that renders `null` leaves the layer click-through (`.pageLayer` sets `pointer-events: none`; its direct children opt back in), so a closed page costs no unmount and no lost conversation state.
- **The entry lives only while its page shows.** `ui-workspace-memory` registers its seat inside the slot's inject lifetime and syncs that registration to its open state, disposing on close and on plugin teardown. A permanent entry that rendered `null` while closed would report the centre as occupied on every blank Session, suppressing the centred hero it is meant to leave alone.
- **`isolation: isolate` on `.centerCol`.** The conversation's own z-indexes reach 100 (its context meter) and 1100 (its stat dialog). Confining them to the column is what lets the page layer win with a declared rank of `1` instead of a number that has to stay ahead of every occupant's — a number nobody can keep current.
- **The seat carries no chrome contract.** Title and layout belong to the page component; `shell.page` supplies only the region and the layering. `ui-workspace-memory` registers `WorkspaceMemorySeat` there, and its page renders the identity row — name, path, description — plus outputs, chats/activity tabs, and the three cards as an ordinary scrolling surface.
- **A page yields the column to any Session opened elsewhere.** The center column's whole point is showing the selected conversation, so a page that stayed would invert the meaning of the click that opened one. Two paths cover it: `ui-workspace`'s chat rows call `workspacePage.close()` before `sessions.open()` — which also handles a click on the chat that is *already* selected, where no state changes for a subscriber to observe — and the page plugin subscribes to the sessions list for every other route (a fork, a dashboard, a create). A cleared selection leaves the page standing: the page is what the no-session view shows.
- **The page and the resident composer divide the track.** An occupying page holds a composer band open beneath its own name and description and publishes the band's box for the seat, and opening a page resolves the Workspace's blank Session so the composer has a live binding. [The live-composer note](2026-09-10-workspace-page-live-composer.md) owns the composer's arrival — it replaced the ask box this note originally placed in the page's own module — and [the composer-band note](2026-09-10-workspace-page-composer-band.md) owns where the two surfaces meet.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep `shell.overlay` and restyle the page full-bleed.** Rejected: the overlay layer is `position: absolute; inset: 0` over the whole frame with `z-index: 20`, so a full-bleed page there necessarily covers the sidebar and the resize handles, and it would need its own mask suppression and pointer-event rules to stop intercepting frame chrome. It also leaves the page unable to reach the conversation's grid geometry, and it keeps the semantic lie: a destination rendered as a floating layer.
- **Register into `conversation` with a higher priority.** Rejected: `conversation` is `single`, so the winner replaces the shipped occupant and every seat that occupant declares (`conversation.session`, `conversation.composer`, `conversation.hero.*`, the input dock) disappears with it. Opening a page would unmount the conversation engine, not hide it.
- **Put the page layer inside `.centerCol`.** Rejected on paint order: a stacking context contains descendants relative to *each other*, so a page layer at rank 1 inside the column still loses to the context meter at rank 100. The layer must be a sibling of the column, and the column must be isolated for that sibling's rank to be meaningful.
- **A router: `history.pushState`, a URL per page, back/forward.** Rejected as a different capability, not a smaller one. The client has no route vocabulary today (navigation is `sessions.current`), and a URL scheme would have to decide persistence, deep linking, and reload semantics for every future page seat. `shell.page` is the composition primitive a router would drive.
- **Keep the page's document-level Escape listener.** Rejected: the page's own `Modal`s already listen for Escape, so one keypress inside the memory preview dismissed both the modal and the page behind it — the page is a surface, not a transient, and it leaves through the surfaces that own the selection.
- **Let the page yield only on the observed selection change.** Rejected as incomplete: clicking the chat that is already selected changes no state, so a subscriber never fires and the click reads as ignored — exactly the report that produced the `close()` verb on the opener service. The subscription alone also cannot see a `sessions.open()` of the current id from anywhere else.
- **Give the ask box the `ui-primitives` button instead of the composer's circle.** Rejected: the request is that the two composers read as one control, and `Button`'s capsule is a different control at a different size on a different token family. The catalog's rule — reuse before restyling, promote on the second consumer — is what a third consumer of this circle should revisit. Moot since [the live-composer note](2026-09-10-workspace-page-live-composer.md): the page renders no input of its own.
- **Reuse the composer by slotting the page's prompt into it.** Rejected here: the composed composer is session-scoped (`conversation.composer`, `session` scope) and drives a live Session's keyboard, draft, and queue; the page's prompt creates a *new* Session in the Workspace. They share an appearance, not a contract. [The live-composer note](2026-09-10-workspace-page-live-composer.md) revisited this once the page could resolve a blank Session of its own: the composer keeps its contract, and the page yields the column to it.

## Consequences

Any feature can now own the center column as a page without touching `ui-conversation`, and the Workspaces navigation column survives opening one. The cost is a fourth frame region whose occupant must keep the page opaque (the CSS module sets a background) and must render `null` rather than an empty shell when it has nothing to show, or it will sit invisibly over the conversation and swallow its clicks. A page is also no longer the sole owner of its own presence: the sidebar closes it through the same Cordis service that opens it, so a page that outlives its subject can be dismissed from outside its own module.

The Workspace page itself is restyled against real ui-theme aliases (`--dsw-alias-bg-base`, `--dsw-alias-bg-layer-*`, `--dsw-alias-label-*`, `--dsw-alias-border-l*`, `--dsw-alias-interactive-bg-hover`, `--dsw-alias-state-error-primary`, `--dsw-alias-markdown-placeholder`), so it follows light/dark like every other feature surface, and it names a class for every element it renders instead of leaving bare `<button>`, `<h2>`, and `<textarea>` at user-agent defaults. Its actions go through the `Button`, `Pill`, and `Input` primitives; its list rows and multi-line editors stay local, which is what the primitive catalog reserves for a genuinely page-specific control.
